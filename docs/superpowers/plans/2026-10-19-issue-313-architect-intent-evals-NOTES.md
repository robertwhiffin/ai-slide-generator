## Headline

- **The v1 architect was already at ceiling before any change.** It was measured on the 4 new cases
  per split plus the 5 existing ones: intent was right on 50/50 rows and positions on 24/24 edit
  rows, train and held-out.
- **The issue's off-by-one did not reproduce.** `edit_request` scored 6/6 (the issue reported 3/5).
  The likely reason: the issue was filed on 2026-10-12, and the prompt's SLIDE NUMBERS rule
  ("`message` 1-indexed; `position`/`target_positions` stay 0-indexed") landed the next day in 5653f3ec.
- **The position fix ships as a contract, not a prompt nudge.**
  - `src/domain/slide_numbering.py` resolves the user's slide numbers to positions in code.
  - `architect_node` sends the result to the model as `slide_numbering` and `slide_references`.
  - The promoted prompt tells the model to copy the positions, never to compute number − 1.
  - Measured after the change: no regression. Intent and positions were 100% on every case, train
    and held-out.
  - A post-review diagnostic ran the promoted prompt on three ad-hoc multi-reference edits. It
    targeted only the slide being changed in all three.

**A2: position references resolve to the right `SlideSpec.position`.**
- *Deterministic:* `src/domain/slide_numbering.py`, 241 table-driven tests.
  - Slide N is the N-th slide in position order over the committed rows (else the persisted spec),
    not N − 1.
  - The grammar covers "slide 2", "slide two", "the second slide", "the 4th slide", "the last/final
    slide", "the penultimate slide", "slides 2-4", "slides 2 to 4", "slides 2, 3 and 5", and
    "slide 2 and 3".
  - Out-of-range numbers are reported, never clamped.
  - Indefinite ordinals ("add a final slide"), hyphenated number words, count-relative phrases and
    counts are deliberately unresolved.
- *Wired into the graph:* `architect_node` sends the two keys (d991b22a).
  - It resolves the latest user turn, not the analyst's synthesis on the re-entry pass.
  - It sends empty references on a describe-only sweeper turn.
  - An accepted edit whose model targets miss every named slide logs a warning; nothing is
    overridden.
- *In the evals:* every case payload carries the keys, built by the same helper. On every edit case
  the resolved positions equal the expected targets (b2a97b42).
- *Prompt:* `slide-refs` is promoted as the v1 default (e4387c83, architect 3ed38cb4c086 → ffc3074dc920).
  - Its SLIDE NUMBERS rule names the keys and says to take positions from them.
  - It says never to compute number − 1, and to answer an out-of-range reference with discuss.
  - It says naming a slide alone does not make a request an edit.
  - Prompt-contract tests pin each of those sentences.
- *Documented for #314:* `CONTEXT.md` (Slide Number, Slide Position, Slide Reference) and
  `docs/technical/backend-overview.md` ("Graph Path: Slide Numbers and Positions", including a
  "For #314" list).

## Decisions made on your behalf

1. **Contract fix over prompt nudge.** Positions are resolved in code and handed to the model. The
   alternative, overriding the model's `target_positions` in the node, was rejected. An off-by-one
   model also revises the wrong `SlideSpec`, and some legitimate edits name one slide and change
   another. The node only logs that case.
2. **What "slide N" means.** It is the N-th slide in position order over the committed rows: what the
   slide panel shows, not `position + 1`. They differ only on a gapped deck. SpecView shows
   `position + 1`, which is left as a follow-up.
3. **Resolve the latest user turn, not `message`.** On the pass after the data analyst returns,
   `message` is the analyst's synthesis. On a describe-only sweeper turn, `slide_references` is
   empty, because the latest user turn predates the hand edit.
4. **The grammar is conservative**, and its documented limits are pinned by tests.
   - Indefinite ordinals ("a final slide", "one last slide") are not references. They name slides
     that do not exist yet, which is #314's to interpret.
   - Count-relative phrases ("the next to last slide") are unresolved rather than misread.
   - Known limit: a bare-comma plural ("On slides 2, 3 bullets each") resolves to [2, 3].
5. **Four cases per split, not more.** At about USD 0.2 per row, the cap allowed about 80 rows in
   total.
   - No add/insert case was added (#314 changes that behaviour).
   - No casual design-switch case (held-out `confirm_design` already is one).
   - No data-needing edit (it is not unambiguous `ask_data` under the current rules).
   - Train `edit_multi_slide` was reworded during authoring so the chart slide in the range keeps its
     chart.
6. **Repeats under the cap.** New cases and edit/confirm/discuss ran ×5 before and after.
   `build_request`/`ask_data` ran ×3: they have no current deck, so only the prompt could move them.
   Held-out ran ×1, the ruled drop step taken when B1's measured cost projected over the cap.
7. **No prompt search.** v1 was at ceiling on every train case, so there were no failures to tune
   against; a search would only have fitted judge noise.
   - The single candidate is the plan's contract text, written before any measurement and containing
     no case content. One sentence was reworded for clarity.
   - It was promoted because the prompt should describe its payload. It is also robust on a gapped
     deck, and out-of-range → discuss is the safe default. It is not promoted for a measured gain.
8. **No price added to `prices.yaml`.** The opus-5 price is not in the repo.
9. **The promoted prompt is kept after the review's multi-reference concern** (finding 2), on the
   diagnostic's 3/3. Rewording it would have invalidated the measured text with about USD 0.3 left.
   Shipping the keys without the prompt would have left gapped decks unprotected.
10. **The resolver abstains rather than guesses** (review finding 1).
    - A singular "slide" followed by a form it cannot read gives no reference:
      - a spaced dash ("slide 2 - 4");
      - "to"/"through"/"thru" and a number ("slide 2 through 5", "move slide 2 to 5");
      - a comma list ("Slide 2, 3 and 4").
    - "slide 2 through / up to / until slide 5" is read as one range.
    - "the second slide from the end", "the last slide but one" and "the last slide deck" give no
      reference.
    - This reverses an earlier supervisor ruling that "slide 2 - 4" meant slide 2. Abstaining is
      safe, because the prompt sends unresolved numbers to `slide_numbering`.
11. **The frontend lifecycle contract fixture is not re-recorded.** That needs PostgreSQL. Precedent
   45ce906c did not re-record it either, and its CI check compares key shape, not prompt text.

## Known gaps and follow-ups

- **Add a two-reference eval case** such as "use slide 3's layout on slide 6" → [5]. The
  multi-reference behaviour is checked only by the 3-row diagnostic.
- **Resolver limits, pinned or documented:**
  - Singular "and <count>": "Put 3 KPIs on slide 2 and 4 on slide 3" reads the 4 as a slide.
  - "to + number" count edits ("Convert slide 4 to 2 columns") now give no reference. That is safe,
    and matches the behaviour before this branch.
  - "the last slide but two" and "the final slide number" still resolve to the last slide.
- **Agent test workbench.** Stored architect cases run without the two keys unless they carry them
  (`model_payload_for` only projects).
- **Rollout.** The Graph V1 manifest seeds a fresh bootstrap only. An existing install gets the
  payload keys at once, but the new SLIDE NUMBERS text only when an admin publishes it.

- **No measured gapped-deck case.** Every eval deck has contiguous positions, so the claim that the
  contract helps on a gapped deck is unit-tested (resolver and node), not live-measured.
- **SpecView's slide label.** It shows `position + 1`; on a gapped deck that disagrees with the panel
  numbering.
- **Unit tests leak MLflow runs** into the worktree's `mlflow.db` (`Default` and untagged
  `tellr-agent-eval` runs with a `safety_gate_retry` param). Pre-existing; not touched.
- **The issue's `build_request` 3/5 evidence** (deck length) is out of scope for intent. It passed
  6/6 here anyway.
- **Not verified locally:** the Postgres integration suites and the lifecycle-contract shape check.
  CI runs them.
