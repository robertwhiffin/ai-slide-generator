The Problem

Ad-hoc prompting silently drains budget, quality, and velocity

Three compounding costs most teams overlook

  • Inconsistent outputs — Without structured prompts, the same query yields wildly different results, forcing manual review and rework every time
  • Wasted tokens & inflated cost — Naive prompts consume 30–50% more tokens than structured alternatives, a gap that scales with every API call
  • Slow iteration cycles — Teams typically need 3–5 revision rounds to reach production-grade quality, burning engineering hours on trial and error
Every unoptimised prompt is a recurring tax — on spend, on consistency, and on time-to-value. These costs compound with every new use case you ship.
